# AutoGuardian AI 🚗🤖

## Edge-Based Vehicle Health and Predictive Maintenance System

AutoGuardian AI is an intelligent vehicle monitoring and predictive maintenance platform designed to improve vehicle safety, health monitoring, and maintenance planning.

The system combines **Edge AI, vehicle health analysis, tyre inspection, driver safety monitoring, and predictive maintenance** into a single web-based platform.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Vehicle Health
Monitors important vehicle parameters and provides an overall vehicle health assessment.

- Engine temperature monitoring
- Battery health monitoring
- Brake wear monitoring
- Engine oil level monitoring
- Coolant level monitoring
- Distance since last service
- Overall vehicle health score
- Component-wise health analysis
- Maintenance risk assessment

---

### 2. TyreVision AI
AI-powered tyre inspection and analysis system.

- Upload tyre images
- Tyre tread analysis
- Tyre wear detection
- Tyre health score
- AI confidence score
- Inspection alerts
- Tyre condition assessment

---

### 3. SafeRide AI
Driver safety monitoring module.

- Driver awareness monitoring
- Driver safety analysis
- Safety alerts
- Risk detection
- Safe driving assistance

---

### 4. AutoCare AI
Predictive maintenance module that analyzes vehicle parameters and helps predict maintenance requirements.

- Vehicle profile management
- Current mileage tracking
- Last service mileage
- Last service date
- Vehicle sensor parameters
- Predictive maintenance recommendations
- Service interval prediction
- Maintenance risk classification

---

### 5. Smart Reports
Provides reports based on vehicle monitoring and AI analysis.

- Vehicle health reports
- Tyre inspection reports
- Maintenance reports
- Performance insights
- AI-generated analysis
- Vehicle monitoring history

---

## 🧠 Edge AI

AutoGuardian AI is designed around an **Edge AI approach**, where vehicle-related data can be analyzed close to the source.

### Benefits of Edge AI

- Low latency
- Real-time processing
- Faster decision making
- Reduced dependency on cloud processing
- Intelligent vehicle monitoring
- Improved response time for safety-related events
